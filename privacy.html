Privacy &
data handling.

Sample copy — have an attorney review to confirm GDPR / CCPA / UK GDPR compliance for your specific business.

1. Information we collect

When you place an order, we collect the personal information you provide directly: name, email address, shipping and billing address, telephone number, organization name (if provided), and the content of any messages you send us. We also collect technical information about your visit (IP address, browser type, device type, pages viewed, referring URL).

2. How we use information

  • To process and fulfill your orders, including shipping and customs documentation.
  • To communicate with you about your order, replies to your inquiries, and important account or policy changes.
  • To prevent fraud and verify research-use eligibility.
  • To improve our website, products, and customer experience.
  • To send you product updates and lab news only if you opt in to our newsletter.

3. Third parties

We share information only with the third parties needed to operate our business: our payment processor, our shipping carriers, our customs broker, our analytical testing lab, and our hosting and email providers. Each is bound by confidentiality and data-protection terms. We do not sell your personal information.

4. Cookies

Our website uses a minimal set of cookies and localStorage to remember your bag, your theme preference, and any session-relevant information. We do not currently use third-party advertising trackers.

5. Data retention

We retain order records for the period required by applicable tax and commerce law (typically seven years). After that period, records are deleted or anonymized. Newsletter subscribers can unsubscribe at any time.

6. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (notably the EU/EEA, UK, and California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or port your personal information, to restrict or object to processing, and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. To exercise any of these rights, email privacy@strand.lab.

7. International transfers

We are an international supplier, and your personal information may be transferred to, and processed in, countries other than your country of residence. Where required, we rely on Standard Contractual Clauses and equivalent safeguards for cross-border transfers.

8. Security

We use industry-standard administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your information. No system is perfectly secure, however — if you believe your account or information has been compromised, contact us immediately.

9. Children

This website and our products are not intended for anyone under 21. We do not knowingly collect information from minors. If you believe we have, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The current version is always on this page.

11. Contact

Privacy questions: privacy@strand.lab. For EU/UK residents, you may also contact our representative at the address provided on request.
