The product

Short chains of amino acids synthesized for use in laboratory research. They are sold as reference materials — for studying molecular biology, biochemistry, and cell signaling in controlled experimental settings. They are not approved by the FDA, EMA, or any other regulatory authority for therapeutic use in humans or animals.

No. STRAND products are sold as laboratory reference materials only. They are not for human consumption, injection, or any therapeutic application. They are not for animal use either. Purchase requires confirmation that you are a qualified researcher.

Every lot is tested by an independent analytical laboratory using HPLC for purity and mass spectrometry for sequence confirmation. Reports are referenced by lot number. After your order arrives, email us with the lot number on your bottle and we'll send the analytical report.

Shipping & customs

Domestic (United States): 1–3 business days. International: 4–10 business days depending on destination and customs clearance. Refrigerated products ship overnight only.

You are responsible for any import duties, customs fees, or taxes imposed by your destination country, and for ensuring these materials are legal to import to your jurisdiction. Some countries restrict or prohibit research chemicals — see our shipping policy for the current restricted list.

If a refrigerated shipment's temperature logger shows a breach, or if a package arrives visibly damaged, we replace it at our cost. Lost packages confirmed by the carrier are also replaced. Contact us within 7 days of expected delivery.
